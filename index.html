❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

Is this mobile responsive for field teams?

Yes — Solar Software is fully mobile-optimized. Field executives use the app to check in, log visits, mark GPS locations, and update lead status from anywhere. Managers see everything live on dashboard.

Can we manage multiple branches and large teams?

Absolutely. The platform scales from 5 to 500+ users. Set up branch-wise views, region-wise reporting, and custom role hierarchies for your exact org structure.

Does it support PM Surya Ghar subsidy & bank workflows?

Yes! Our platform includes bank loan application tracking, document management, verification status, and disbursement milestone monitoring — specifically designed for India's solar subsidy ecosystem.

How does GPS tracking work on the field agent's phone?

Field agents use the Solar Software mobile app. It runs GPS in the background, auto-records location at customer sites, captures geo-fenced check-in/check-out, and sends daily route summaries to managers — without agents needing to do anything manually.

How accurate is the AI face recognition attendance?

99.7%+ accuracy under normal conditions. The AI adapts to lighting variations and partial obstructions. All records are timestamped with location, making it legally verifiable and completely fraud-proof.

Is role-based access control available?

Yes. Create custom roles — Field Executive, TL, Branch Manager, Regional Head, HQ Admin — each with precisely defined data visibility and action permissions.

What integrations do you support?

Solar Software integrates with WhatsApp Business, Razorpay & PayU payment gateways, major banks for loan tracking, Tally for accounting, and Zapier for custom automation workflows.

How secure is my business data?

Enterprise-grade 256-bit AES encryption, daily automated backups, VAPT-tested infrastructure, and SOC2-compliant hosting. Your data is safer with us than on a local server or spreadsheet.
