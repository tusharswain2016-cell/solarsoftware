What Is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government scheme under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) that provides direct financial subsidies for rooftop solar installation to residential consumers across India. The scheme is designed to reduce electricity bills for households — and ultimately provide up to 300 units of free electricity per month to eligible families — by making solar installation affordable through upfront capital subsidies.

The scheme operates through a centralised national portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) and works in coordination with Distribution Companies (DISCOMs) in each state. The subsidy amount is determined by the system capacity installed and is disbursed directly to the customer's bank account after successful installation and inspection — it is not paid to the installer. This is an important distinction that every installer needs to communicate clearly to customers during the sales process.

ℹ️
Key Point for Installers The PM Surya Ghar subsidy is disbursed directly to the customer's Aadhaar-linked bank account — not to your company. You cannot deduct the subsidy upfront from your invoice. The customer pays your full price and receives the subsidy separately. Make this absolutely clear before signing any agreement.

Customer Eligibility Criteria

Not every homeowner qualifies for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Understanding the eligibility criteria before the site visit saves both your team and the customer significant time and prevents expectations being set that cannot be met.

Who Qualifies for PM Surya Ghar Subsidy

  • Residential consumers only: The scheme is exclusively for households. Commercial, industrial, or agricultural consumers are not eligible for this central scheme subsidy.
  • Owner-occupied premises: The applicant must be the owner of the property where the system is to be installed, with the electricity connection in their name.
  • Valid electricity connection: The customer must have an existing, active DISCOM electricity connection at the installation address.
  • No prior central subsidy: Households that have previously received a central government subsidy for rooftop solar are not eligible to apply again.
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account: The subsidy is disbursed via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) — the customer must have a bank account linked to their Aadhaar number.
  • DISCOM registration: The customer must register on the national portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) and receive DISCOM approval before installation begins — post-installation applications are not accepted.
  • Empanelled installer only: The installation must be carried out by an installer empanelled on the national portal. DIY or un-empanelled installations do not qualify.
⚠️
Flat / Apartment Customers Residents in housing societies and apartment complexes face additional complexity. The installation may require housing society approval, and the electricity connection must be in the individual flat owner's name — not a common meter. Always verify this during lead qualification to avoid wasted site visits.

Vendor Empanelment: How to Register as an Approved Installer

To be eligible to install systems under PM Surya Ghar and enable your customers to claim the subsidy, your company must be registered as an empanelled vendor on the national portal. Non-empanelled installations — even if technically excellent — do not qualify for the central subsidy, and customers who use non-empanelled installers will lose their subsidy entitlement entirely.

1

Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in

Visit the national portal and click "Register as Vendor." You will need your company's GST number, PAN, Udyam/MSME registration (if applicable), bank account details for payments, and a list of the states/districts where you intend to operate. The registration is free of charge.

2

Submit Technical Credentials

Upload documents demonstrating your technical capability: electrical contractor licence, details of qualified electrical engineers on staff, and a portfolio of previous installations (if available). The MNRE portal verifies these credentials before approving empanelment — the process typically takes 7–15 working days.

3

Register with Your State DISCOM(s)

In addition to the national portal, most states also require installers to register separately with the state DISCOM. This is because the DISCOM is responsible for technical inspection, net meter installation, and final approval. Contact your state DISCOM's renewable energy department directly — registration requirements and timelines vary significantly by state.

4

Keep Your Empanelment Current

Empanelment is not permanent — it requires periodic renewal and compliance with updated MNRE technical standards. Set a reminder to review your empanelment status every 6 months. A lapse in empanelment means your customers cannot claim subsidies, which is both a legal and reputational risk for your business.

The End-to-End Installation & Subsidy Process

Understanding the full process — from the customer's initial registration to the final subsidy disbursement — is essential for setting accurate timelines with customers and managing your own project pipeline effectively. The process has 7 key stages:

1

Customer Registers on National Portal

The customer registers on pmsuryaghar.gov.in with their electricity consumer number and Aadhaar. This can be done by the customer themselves or assisted by your team — many of the best solar companies provide this as a value-added service during the site visit, removing friction and accelerating the process. Registration takes 10–15 minutes.

2

DISCOM Feasibility Approval

After registration, the customer's local DISCOM reviews the application and grants feasibility approval — confirming that the grid can accommodate the proposed solar system. This typically takes 7–30 days depending on the state and DISCOM's workload. This step must be completed before installation begins. Starting work before DISCOM approval will disqualify the subsidy.

3

Installation by Empanelled Vendor

Once DISCOM approval is received, your team proceeds with installation. Use components that comply with MNRE specifications — BIS-certified panels and MNRE-approved inverters. Non-compliant equipment is a common reason for technical rejection during inspection. Document the entire installation with timestamped photos in your CRM for easy submission.

4

Technical Inspection by DISCOM

After installation, the customer notifies the DISCOM through the portal. A DISCOM technical officer visits to inspect the installation and verify compliance. This inspection can take anywhere from 3 days to 6 weeks depending on the state — Maharashtra and Gujarat are typically faster; some UP and MP districts have longer wait times. Be transparent with customers about realistic timelines.

5

Net Meter Installation

After passing inspection, the DISCOM installs a bidirectional net meter to measure both energy consumed from the grid and energy exported to it. This is the DISCOM's responsibility and cost — your team does not install the net meter. However, your installation must include the correct metering point preparation as per DISCOM specifications. Net meter installation timelines are the most variable part of the process: 2 weeks to 3 months in different states.

6

Commissioning Certificate & Portal Update

Once the net meter is installed and the system is live, the DISCOM issues a commissioning certificate. This is uploaded to the portal by either the DISCOM or the installer (depending on state). The system is now officially commissioned and the subsidy claim is triggered.

7

Subsidy Disbursement to Customer's Bank Account

Within 30 days of successful commissioning confirmation, the central government subsidy is transferred directly to the customer's Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT. The customer does not need to take any further action — the transfer is automatic once the system is commissioned. Advise customers to keep their bank account active and ensure their Aadhaar-bank linkage is verified before the process begins.

Using PM Surya Ghar as a Sales Tool — The Right Way

The subsidy is a powerful sales accelerator — but only if used correctly. Solar companies that handle the scheme conversation poorly create more problems than they solve: customers who believe the subsidy reduces the upfront price, customers who expect unrealistically short timelines, and customers who feel misled when the process takes longer than expected.

The right way to use PM Surya Ghar in your sales conversation has three components. First, lead with the financial benefit clearly and accurately: "The government will transfer ₹78,000 directly to your bank account after the system is commissioned — this is not a discount on our price, but it significantly reduces your net cost." This framing sets accurate expectations from the start.

Second, position your company's process expertise as a differentiator: "We handle the entire portal registration, document submission, and DISCOM follow-up on your behalf — you do not need to do anything except provide your documents." Many customers are intimidated by government paperwork, and removing that friction is a real competitive advantage.

Third, be honest about timelines: "The subsidy arrives in your account approximately 8–16 weeks after we complete the installation, depending on your DISCOM's current processing speed. We will keep you updated at every stage." Customers who receive accurate timelines and regular updates stay satisfied. Customers who were told "it will come in 4 weeks" and are still waiting at 10 weeks become your biggest source of negative word-of-mouth.

Common Mistakes Installers Make With the Scheme

After working with hundreds of solar installers across India on PM Surya Ghar applications, these are the mistakes that consistently cause subsidy rejections, delays, and customer complaints:

  • Starting installation before DISCOM feasibility approval: The most common and most expensive mistake. The subsidy is categorically denied if work begins before the DISCOM grants approval. No exceptions.
  • Using non-MNRE-approved equipment: Panels or inverters that are not on the MNRE approved list will fail technical inspection. Always verify your equipment list against the current MNRE portal before purchasing for PM Surya Ghar jobs.
  • Misrepresenting the subsidy as an upfront discount: Telling customers "you only need to pay ₹87,000" — implying the subsidy is deducted at purchase — is legally and ethically incorrect. The full price must be paid, and the subsidy comes later. Companies that misrepresent this face customer complaints and, in serious cases, DISCOM blacklisting.
  • Submitting incomplete documentation: Missing documents are the single biggest cause of application delays. A digital checklist enforced at the site visit stage eliminates this problem entirely.
  • Failing to update the portal after installation: The installer is responsible for updating the national portal after installation is complete. Forgetting this step delays the DISCOM inspection trigger and pushes back the entire timeline.
  • Operating in districts not covered by your empanelment: Your empanelment covers specific geographic areas. Installing outside your registered coverage area invalidates the subsidy claim. Always verify coverage before accepting a job in a new district.

Frequently Asked Questions from Customers

These are the questions your sales and operations teams will hear most often from customers about PM Surya Ghar. Having confident, accurate answers to all of them is a direct competitive advantage.

Will I get the subsidy before or after installation?
The subsidy is disbursed after installation. The process is: (1) register on the portal, (2) get DISCOM feasibility approval, (3) installation, (4) DISCOM technical inspection, (5) net meter installation, (6) system commissioning, and finally (7) subsidy credited to your bank account. You pay the full installation cost to us, and then receive the subsidy in your bank account — typically within 30 days of commissioning.
Can a tenant apply for the subsidy?
No. The subsidy is available only to the property owner whose name appears on the electricity bill. A tenant cannot apply, even with the landlord's permission. The electricity connection and the property ownership must both be in the applicant's name.
What if I already have solar installed on my roof?
If you previously received a central government subsidy for your existing system, you are not eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy again. However, if you installed solar without any government subsidy, you may be eligible — check the portal to verify your previous application status against your consumer number.
How long does the entire process take?
The timeline varies by state and DISCOM. In faster states like Gujarat and Delhi, the complete process from registration to subsidy disbursement typically takes 8–12 weeks. In states with higher workloads like UP or Tamil Nadu, 14–20 weeks is more realistic. We will give you a state-specific timeline estimate during your consultation and keep you updated throughout the process.
Is the 300 units free electricity really free?
The "free electricity" refers to the energy your solar system generates and offsets from your grid consumption through net metering. If your 3 kWp system generates 300–360 units per month and your consumption is 300 units, your effective net bill from the DISCOM is zero or minimal. It is not a direct cash transfer — it works through the savings on your electricity bill via net metering. The exact savings depend on your consumption pattern and your DISCOM's net metering tariff.
Can I get a loan to cover the upfront cost before the subsidy arrives?
Yes — this is strongly encouraged by the scheme. Several nationalised banks and small finance banks offer dedicated PM Surya Ghar solar loans at subsidised interest rates. We can connect you with our banking partners for a quick approval. Many customers choose to take a short-term loan, repay it when the subsidy arrives, and then enjoy pure electricity savings from the system.
